#6 Most Popular Food in the World

Tacos

Tacos are a traditional Mexican dish made with folded tortillas filled with meat, vegetables, cheese, and sauces.

They are incredibly popular worldwide and come in many different styles and flavours depending on the region.

Why People Love Tacos

People love tacos because they are versatile, easy to customise, and packed with flavour in every bite.

Fun Fact: Tacos are believed to have originated in Mexico long before the Spanish arrived!

#1 Most Popular Food in the World

Pizza

Pizza is one of the most famous foods in the world, originating from Italy and now enjoyed globally in countless styles and flavours.

It typically consists of a baked dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a wide variety of toppings such as meats and vegetables.

Why People Love Pizza

People love pizza because it is easy to share, highly customisable, and packed with flavour in every slice.

Fun Fact: The world’s first pizza is believed to have been made in Naples, Italy!

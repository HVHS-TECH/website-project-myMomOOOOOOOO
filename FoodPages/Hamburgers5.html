#5 Most Popular Food in the World

Hamburgers

Hamburgers are one of the most popular fast foods in the world, made with a grilled beef patty inside a soft bun.

They are often topped with cheese, lettuce, tomato, onions, and sauces, making them highly customisable and widely loved.

Why People Love Hamburgers

People love hamburgers because they are quick, filling, and can be made in many different styles and flavours.

Fun Fact: The modern hamburger became popular in the United States in the early 1900s!

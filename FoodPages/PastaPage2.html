#2 Most Popular Food in the World

Pasta

Pasta is a traditional Italian food made from wheat dough and shaped into many different forms like spaghetti, penne, and macaroni.

It is often served with sauces such as tomato, cream, or cheese-based recipes, making it a versatile and loved dish worldwide.

Why People Love Pasta

People love pasta because it is simple, filling, and can be paired with almost any type of sauce or ingredient.

Fun Fact: Italy has hundreds of different pasta shapes used in different dishes!

#7 Most Popular Food in the World

Fried Chicken

Fried chicken is a popular dish known for its crispy, golden coating and juicy meat inside.

It is seasoned, coated in batter or breadcrumbs, and deep-fried until crispy, making it a favourite comfort food worldwide.

Why People Love Fried Chicken

People love fried chicken for its crunch, flavour, and satisfying texture. It is one of the most popular fast foods in the world.

Fun Fact: Fried chicken became globally popular after spreading from Southern American cuisine!

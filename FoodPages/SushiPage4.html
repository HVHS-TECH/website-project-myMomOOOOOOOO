#4 Most Popular Food in the World

Sushi

Sushi is a traditional Japanese dish made with vinegared rice combined with seafood, vegetables, or other ingredients.

It is known for its fresh ingredients, artistic presentation, and wide variety of styles such as rolls, nigiri, and sashimi.

Why People Love Sushi

People love sushi because it is fresh, light, and visually appealing, offering a unique dining experience.

Fun Fact: Sushi originally began as a method of preserving fish in fermented rice!
